        Complete Feature Specification of Personal Health OS & Care Journey Network

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Healthcare delivery worldwide suffers from a catastrophic divide: the gap between 
an individual's daily personal health management and institutional hospital care. 
Patients manage chronic illnesses in isolation with passive tools, while hospitals 
and doctors operate within disconnected organizational silos.

MediTrack eliminates this divide through a unified, **Two-Pillar Architecture**:

  1. PILLAR I: THE PERSONAL HEALTH RECORD (PHR) & AUTONOMOUS PATIENT OS
     A 24/7 background guardian engine that automates medication adherence, parses 
     complex lab reports with AI, tracks women's reproductive health, evaluates 
     Ayurvedic nutrition, manages family emergency escalations, and secures national 
     digital health identities (ABDM).

  2. PILLAR II: THE CARE JOURNEY & CONNECTED HOSPITAL/DOCTOR NETWORK
     An enterprise clinical network unifying Primary Healthcare Centers (PHCs), 
     Community Health Centers (CHCs), District Hospitals, multi-hospital doctor mobility, 
     bed-locked emergency transfers, digital referrals, live OPD queues, diagnostic 
     labs, and LiveKit HD teleconsultations with post-session follow-up quotas.

This monograph presents an exhaustive, feature-by-feature specification of all 
modules, engines, workflows, and global standards implemented in MediTrack.

SECTION 1: THE PERSONAL HEALTH RECORD (PHR) & PATIENT OS SYSTEM
================================================================================

The Personal Health Pillar transforms the patient experience from passive manual 
logging into a continuous, AI-powered health operating system.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Autonomous Living Health Guardian Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background Asynchronous Intelligence: Powered by Redis and BullMQ worker queues, 
  MediTrack continuously monitors health trajectories offline without requiring the app to be open.
- Weighted Living Health Score Algorithm (0–100):
    Health Score = (0.60 * Adherence Rate) + (0.30 * Sleep Score) + (0.10 * Vitals Indicator)
- Health State Tiers:
    * 🟢 Stable (Green Tier - Score > 80): Optimal medication routine, healthy sleep gap indicators.
    * 🟡 Attention Needed (Yellow Tier - Score 50–80): Minor adherence slips, erratic sleep trends.
    * 🔴 Risk Detected (Red Tier - Score < 50): Severe non-adherence, abnormal blood pressure/glucose.
- Automated 3-Hour Emergency Family Escalation: When a patient enters a critical 
  Red Alert state and remains in Red for 3 consecutive hours, MediTrack dispatches 
  automated SMS and Email alerts to designated family guardians with actionable clinical summaries.
- Passive Sleep Intelligence Engine: Infers sleep duration and sleep quality from passive 
  user activity gap logs (time between last night activity and morning login).
- Weekly Trajectory & Improvement Detector: Compares 7-day trailing metrics to identify 
  positive routine improvements and issue positive reinforcement alerts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 AI Medicine Management, Folder Organization & Drug Catalog
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Medicine Scheduling & Dosage Tracker: Configures drug names, dosages, frequencies, 
  specific times, duration, stock quantities, minimum thresholds, and food timing 
  (before/after meals).
- Smart Grace-Period Reminders: Dispatches push and email alerts when doses are due, 
  tracking grace-period overdue windows before marking doses as missed.
- AI Therapeutic Folder Auto-Categorization: Automatically sorts user medicines into 
  18 therapeutic folders:
    1. Pain Relief            7. Digestive Health       13. Mental Health
    2. Fever                  8. Nausea & Vomiting      14. Vitamins & Supplements
    3. Anti-inflammatory      9. Allergy                15. Skin Care
    4. Antibiotic            10. Respiratory            16. Eye Care
    5. Antiviral             11. Cardiovascular         17. Hormonal
    6. Antifungal            12. Diabetes               18. Antacid
- Multi-Tier Drug Intelligence Pipeline: Queries OpenFDA API -> RxNorm API -> Gemini 
  Web Scraping Fallback to extract indications, precautions, and food interactions.
- 90-Day Lookup Cache (`MedicineLookupCache`): Caches drug API responses for 90 days, 
  reducing external LLM API calls by >80%.
- Confidence Scoring & Manual Overrides: Displays confidence ratings for auto-categorizations 
  and allows drag-and-drop manual folder re-assignment.
- Drug Interaction & Food Reaction Warnings: Flags dangerous drug-drug combinations and 
  food-drug conflicts (e.g., avoiding dairy with certain antibiotics).
- Inventory Expiry & Stock Replenishment Alerts: Scans batch numbers and stock thresholds, 
  alerting users when medicines are near expiry or low in stock.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 Smart Lab Report OCR & Gemini Clinical Thesis Synthesizer
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Encrypted Cloud Storage: Uploads PDF and image lab reports to Cloudinary with AES-256 
  encryption.
- Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Parses unstructured lab reports using Tesseract.js 
  and Google Gemini 2.5 Flash to extract numerical biomarkers and reference ranges.
- Domain Risk Categorization: Automatically sorts lab metrics into Cardiology, 
  Endocrinology, Nephrology, and General Health panels.
- Longitudinal Clinical Health Thesis Synthesizer: Aggregates historical lab reports 
  over months/years into a single, 1-page clinical summary for attending physicians.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 Women's Health & PCOS/PCOD Trend Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Comprehensive Cycle Tracker: Logs cycle start/end dates, flow intensity, physical 
  symptoms, mood variations, and pain levels.
- Cycle Irregularity Algorithm: Analyzes inter-cycle date gaps:
    * Stable: 21–35 days
    * Monitor: 36–45 days
    * High Irregularity: >45 days
    * Critical Alert: >=90 days (Triggers PCOS/PCOD medical assessment alerts)
- Cycle Phase Forecasting: Predicts Follicular, Luteal, Ovulation, and Menstrual phases.
- Interactive Recharts Visualizers: Area charts displaying cycle length trends and 
  symptom severity over time.
- Reproductive Wellness Guidance: Delivers customized lifestyle recommendations based 
  on cycle phase and irregularity score.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.5 Food, Nutrition & Ayurvedic Prakriti Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- AI Photo Meal Scanner: Analyzes meal photographs using Gemini Vision to estimate calorie 
  counts, macronutrients, glycemic load, and chronic disease risk factors.
- Ayurvedic Prakriti Assessment: Evaluates Vata, Pitta, and Kapha dosha balances via 
  a clinical quiz to identify the user's dominant Prakriti type.
- Integrated Wellness Plan: Combines traditional Ayurvedic dietary guidelines with modern 
  clinical nutrition advice.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.6 Family Guardian Network & Caregiver Portal
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Family Member Linking: Connects family members, elderly parents, and children under 
  a single guardian umbrella.
- Remote Health Score Visibility: Allows family guardians to monitor real-time health 
  scores and adherence percentages of aging relatives remotely.
- Emergency Alert Dispatch: Sends instant SMS and Email notifications during 3-hour 
  persistent Red Alert states.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.7 Government Digital Health & ABDM Integration
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 14-Digit ABHA ID Management: Generates, links, and verifies standard Ayushman Bharat 
  Health Account numbers (`XX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).
- Custom `@abdm` PHR Address: Assigns unique personal health handles (e.g., `rahul@abdm`).
- Embedded ABDM QR Code Card: Generates client-side QR codes embedded with structured 
  JSON payloads for rapid, contact-free hospital check-in scans.
- Granular Consent Artifacts (`AbdmConsentRequest`): Empowers patients to review, grant, 
  scope (sharing only Prescriptions, Lab Reports, Diagnostic Reports, or Health Summaries), 
  or revoke hospital data access requests with automated `validTill` expiration enforcement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.8 Daily Health Review, Checkups & AI Health Assistant
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Daily Review Logger: Captures daily mood, energy levels, symptom logs, and overall wellness.
- Preventive Checkup Reminders: Schedules routine blood pressure, blood glucose, and 
  annual health screening checkups.
- Gemini AI Health Assistant Chat: Conversational AI assistant for instant medical Q&A, 
  symptom guidance, and app navigation assistance.
- User Profile & Security Settings: Manages personal demographics, biometric security, 
  and notification channel preferences (Email, SMS, Push).

SECTION 2: THE CARE JOURNEY & CONNECTED HOSPITAL/DOCTOR NETWORK
================================================================================

The Care Journey Pillar transforms isolated medical facilities into an integrated, 
multi-tier clinical care network.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Multi-Tier Healthcare Facility Hierarchy & Onboarding
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Facility Classification: Onboards Primary Healthcare Centers (PHCs), Community 
  Health Centers (CHCs), District Hospitals, Tertiary Teaching Hospitals, and Private 
  Specialty Clinics.
- Facility Profile & Verification: Captures facility license IDs, government registration, 
  address, GIS map coordinates, emergency services availability, and operational departments.
- System Admin Verification Workflow: System administrators review and verify facility 
  and doctor credentials before granting network access.
- Security Audit Logging (`CareAuditLog`): Immutable audit trail recording administrative 
  actions, doctor verifications, and patient record accesses.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 Dynamic Multi-Hospital Doctor Mobility (`DoctorFacilityAssociation`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Decoupled Doctor Identity: Medical doctors maintain independent professional profiles 
  with medical registration numbers, licensing authorities, specializations, qualifications, 
  and experience years.
- Multi-Hospital Association Lifecycle: Doctors request and maintain dynamic links 
  across multiple facilities (`PENDING` -> `ACTIVE` -> `SUSPENDED` / `ENDED`).
- One-Click Facility Switching: Doctors switch facility dashboards with one click—managing 
  OPD queues, accepting referrals, and reviewing inpatient charts across different 
  hospitals without losing schedule or patient history context.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 Real-Time Bed-Locked Emergency Transfer Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-Time Bed Capacity Engine (`FacilityCapacity`): Tracks live availability for:
    * Emergency Room Beds           * Oxygen-Supported Beds
    * General Ward Beds             * Available Ventilator Units
    * Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Beds
- Bed-Locked Transfer Workflow (`PatientTransfer`): Originating facility requests an 
  emergency transfer specifying required bed type (e.g., ICU with Ventilator) and 
  clinical summary. The receiving hospital reviews and accepts the request, locking 
  the required bed in real time before the ambulance departs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 Digital Inter-Hospital Referral Pipeline (`CareReferral`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Digital Referral Creation: Rural PHC medical officers digitally refer patients up 
  the care chain (PHC -> CHC -> District Hospital).
- Attached Care Journey Timeline (`CareJourneyTimelineEvent`): Automatically attaches the 
  patient's longitudinal timeline to the referral.
- Context-Preserved Clinical Triage: Receiving specialists access all historical lab 
  reports, prescriptions, and vital trends instantly, eliminating paper loss and 
  redundant diagnostic testing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 Live OPD Queue Token System (`CareQueue`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- OPD Queue Creation: Facilities and doctors establish sequential daily OPD token queues.
- Sequential Check-In: Patients check in digitally or at facility desks to receive a 
  sequential token number.
- Live Queue State Progression: Doctors advance queue states (`NEXT`, `COMPLETE`, `SKIP`) 
  from their clinical dashboards.
- Mobile Live Position Updates: Live queue tokens broadcast instantly to patient 
  smartphones, allowing patients to wait comfortably outside until their turn arrives.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6 LiveKit HD Teleconsultation & 10 Post-Session Follow-Up Message Quota
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Low-Latency HD Telemedicine: Adaptive WebRTC video consultations powered by LiveKit 
  Cloud SDK, operating smoothly even on low-bandwidth rural 2G/3G networks.
- Teleconsultation Lifecycle Management: Appointment booking, token generation, socket 
  room joining, and call termination (`TERMINATED`).
- 10 Post-Session Follow-Up Message Quota: When a doctor terminates a teleconsultation, 
  MediTrack automatically grants the patient 10 follow-up text or audio clip messages 
  (`postSessionMessagesLeft: 10`). Patients can clarify dosage queries or report side 
  effects within a protected post-session channel (`CareMessage`) without paying for 
  new appointments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.7 Outpatient Appointments & Digital Prescriptions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Appointment Scheduling: Manages in-person and teleconsultation bookings, time slots, 
  token generation, and status workflows (`SCHEDULED`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`).
- Digital Prescription Generator: Doctors issue digital prescriptions with drug dosages, 
  frequencies, duration, administration instructions, and attached diagnostic orders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.8 Diagnostic Lab Orders & Public Pharmacy Inventory Search
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Care Diagnostic Orders (`CareDiagnosticOrder`): Doctors issue lab orders directly from 
  the consultation desk; lab technicians execute tests and upload verified PDF reports 
  directly to the patient's record.
- Public Medicine Inventory Search (`CareMedicineInventory`): Patients search nearby 
  hospital and clinic inventory stock levels in real time to locate rare medications 
  during supply shortages.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.9 Longitudinal Care Journey Timeline (`CareJourneyTimelineEvent`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Unified Clinical Timeline: Automatically records every clinical event—appointments, 
  prescriptions, lab results, referrals, emergency transfers, teleconsultations, and 
  ABDM consent approvals—into a single, synchronized timeline for lifetime care continuity.

SECTION 3: GLOBAL STANDARDS & HL7 FHIR R4 COMPLIANCE ROADMAP
================================================================================

MediTrack bridges both pillars using international HL7 FHIR R4 (Fast Healthcare 
Interoperability Resources) specifications for global EHR interoperability:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pillar I (Personal Health) FHIR Mapping
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Patient Profile & ABHA ID     ==> FHIR `Patient` Resource
  - Daily Vitals & Biomarkers     ==> FHIR `Observation` Resource
  - Prescriptions & Doses         ==> FHIR `MedicationRequest` Resource
  - ABDM Consent Artifacts        ==> FHIR `Consent` Resource

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pillar II (Care Journey) FHIR Mapping
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Doctor Profiles               ==> FHIR `Practitioner` Resource
  - Facilities & Bed Capacity     ==> FHIR `Location` & `Organization` Resources
  - OPD Appointments & Tokens     ==> FHIR `Encounter` & `Appointment` Resources
  - Inter-Hospital Referrals      ==> FHIR `ServiceRequest` Resource
  - Emergency Transfer Locks      ==> FHIR `Task` & `Transfer` Resources
  - Diagnostic Lab Orders         ==> FHIR `DiagnosticReport` Resource

4.2 Technology Stack Matrix
  - Patient Frontend: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion (glassmorphic Aura UI), 
    Lucide Icons, Recharts (`/frontend`, Port 5173).
  - Provider Frontend: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS (`/care_frontend`, Port 5174).
  - Backend Microservices: Node.js, Express.js microservices (`/backend`, Port 5000 & 
    `/care_backend`, Port 5001) with JWT authentication and bcrypt password security.
  - Asynchronous Queues: Redis Cloud / Upstash with BullMQ (`backend/src/living-os/`) 
    executing 5 background worker queues.
  - AI & Vision: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash / Lite, Tesseract.js OCR, OpenFDA & RxNorm drug 
    APIs with a 90-day lookup cache (`MedicineLookupCache`).
  - Real-Time Video Telemedicine: LiveKit Cloud SDK (WebRTC adaptive bitrate) with 10 
    post-session follow-up messages (`postSessionMessagesLeft: 10`).
  - Database Tier: MongoDB Atlas modeling 40+ synchronized entities.
  - Cost Overhead: Estimated at ~$130–$290 (approx. ₹10,600–₹24,200) monthly for 10,000 
    active users and 50 clinics (~₹2 to ₹3 per active patient per month).
